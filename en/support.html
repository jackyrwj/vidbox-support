FAQ

  • Saving to Photos fails: allow "Add Photos Only" in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Jianxia.
  • Auto Subtitles finds nothing: allow Jianxia in Settings → Privacy & Security → Speech Recognition. The first run waits for iOS to download the model.
  • Enhance is unavailable: Enhance requires iOS 26 or later.
  • Restore purchases: tap Settings at the top right of the home screen, then Restore Purchases.
  • Cancel a subscription: Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Jianxia. Pro stays active until the end of the current period.
  • Refunds: handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
