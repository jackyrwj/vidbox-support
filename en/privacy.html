Breezecut Privacy Policy

Effective: October 7, 2026

Breezecut ("the app") respects your privacy. The app has no accounts, contains no advertising, analytics or third-party data collection SDKs, and the developer runs no server that receives your data.

Your videos, photos and audio

Videos, photos, GIFs and music you choose are processed only on your device and are never uploaded. Temporary files created during processing stay in the app's temporary folder and are cleaned up by the system.

Speech recognition and enhancement

Auto Subtitles uses Apple's built-in on-device speech recognition. Enhance and Face Blur use Apple's built-in on-device image models. Audio and frames never leave your device. The first time you use them, iOS may download a model; this is handled by iOS.

Permissions

  • Photos (add only): to save exported videos, images, GIFs and Live Photos to your library. The app can only read items you pick in the system picker.
  • Speech recognition: only to turn speech in a video into subtitles, on device.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by the Apple App Store. Apple processes payment; the developer never sees your payment method or Apple Account. The app only asks Apple, on your device, whether your purchase is active. A small usage record (such as how many free tries of a Pro tool you have used) is stored only on your device.

Sharing and deletion

The app does not sell, rent or share any of your data. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
