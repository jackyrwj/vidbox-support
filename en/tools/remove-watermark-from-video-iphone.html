Remove a watermark or logo from a video on iPhone

A station logo in the corner, a username stamp, or old captions you don't want: Breezecut's Remove Watermark tool covers them on your iPhone. Please only use it on videos you have the right to edit.

Steps

  1. Open Breezecut and tap Remove Watermark.
  2. Pick a video and drag a box over the watermark.
  3. Choose how to cover it: blur, pixelate, or fill from the surrounding picture.
  4. Add more boxes if needed, set when each one appears, then export.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, Remove Watermark is free and adds no watermark of its own.

Does it work on moving logos?

Each box can have its own start and end time, so you can follow a logo that changes position.

Is my video uploaded?

No, everything is processed on the iPhone.
