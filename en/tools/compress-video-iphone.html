How to compress a video on iPhone without uploading it

Videos from the iPhone camera are huge, and many chat apps and email services reject files over a size limit. Breezecut's Compress tool shrinks a video right on your phone: nothing is uploaded to a website, and the result has no watermark.

Steps

  1. Open Breezecut and tap Compress (or search “compress” on the home screen).
  2. Pick a video from your photo library or Files.
  3. Choose a preset. HEVC keeps the most quality at the smallest size; H.264 plays everywhere, including older devices and Windows PCs.
  4. Check the estimated size, tap Export, then Save to Photos or Share.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Compress is free, unlimited and adds no watermark.

Will my video be uploaded?

No. Compression runs on your iPhone with Apple's own video encoder.

How much smaller will it get?

It depends on the source, but a 4K clip saved as 1080p HEVC is often 70–90% smaller.
