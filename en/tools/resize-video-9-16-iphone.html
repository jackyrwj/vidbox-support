Resize a video to 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9 on iPhone (no black bars)

A landscape video posted to a vertical feed ends up tiny, with black bars. Breezecut's Resize tool changes the canvas to 9:16, 1:1, 16:9 and more, and fills the empty space with a blurred copy of the video.

Steps

  1. Open Breezecut and tap Resize.
  2. Pick a video and choose the aspect ratio.
  3. Keep the whole picture (Fit) and fill the space with a blurred background, black or white, or choose Fill to crop instead.
  4. Export and post.

Only if you want it to. Fit keeps the whole picture and adds a background; Fill crops to the new shape.
