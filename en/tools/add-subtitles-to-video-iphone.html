Add subtitles to a video on iPhone automatically

Most people watch short videos with the sound off, so captions matter. Breezecut listens to your video and writes the subtitles for you using Apple's on-device speech recognition. The audio never leaves your phone.

Steps

  1. Open Breezecut and tap Auto Subtitles.
  2. Pick a video and choose the spoken language.
  3. Wait a moment while the speech is recognized, then fix any line by tapping it.
  4. Export an SRT file, or burn the subtitles into the video with your chosen style and size.

FAQ

Is it free?

Recognizing, editing and exporting subtitles as an SRT file is free. Burning them into the video is a Pro feature with one free try.

Is my audio uploaded?

No. Recognition runs on device. The first time you use a language, iOS may download its speech model itself.

Which languages work?

The languages supported by Apple's on-device speech recognition, including English and Chinese.
